  `<strategy_py>.json` with EXACT structure {"strategy_name":..., "params": params,
  "ft_stratparam_v": 1, "export_time": <UTC ISO>}.
- `read_params_file(path)` -> inner "params" dict.
- `perturb_params(params, pct, *, rng)`: deep-copy walk. bool/None/str unchanged; int v>0 ->

  dict/list recurse; delta = rng.uniform(-pct, +pct). Deterministic for a seeded rng.
